The Nando's University Rankings

Forget the Times Higher Education rankings, the Complete University Guide, and the Research Excellence Framework.

There’s a far more important measure of institutional quality: how close are you to a Nando’s?

Introducing the Nando’s Excellence Framework (NEF) - the definitive ranking of UK universities based on their average distance from a cheeky Nando’s.

Using official university location data from the HESA Discover Uni dataset, OpenStreetMap Nando’s locations, and a frankly excessive amount of help from Claude AI, we’ve calculated the straight-line distance from every university campus to its nearest Nando’s, before averaging the results for each institution (based on UKPRN).

Most campuses are included, the distances have been crunched, and the rankings have been produced.

We did, admittedly, remove a few institutions along the way. No particular reason. Just didn't fancy them being there. If you're missing, shout at us on social media.

The methodology is rigorous. The academic value is questionable. The peri-peri implications are enormous.
