The University Scrabble Score Rankings

Introducing our groundbreaking higher education league table: The University Scrabble Score Rankings – where every letter counts!

Our methodology is faultless. Bantshire’s leading data experts have painstakingly placed every UK university on the Scrabble board and ranked them according to their letter value. No consideration has been given to teaching quality, research excellence, student satisfaction or anything else remotely relevant.

Before you complain… our list of universities comes from HESA. If you’re unhappy with your university’s name, please take it up with them. We merely crunch the letters.
